Our Story & Philosophy

"A daily celebration of terroir, craftsmanship, and timeless café hospitality."

EST. 2018
Independent & Family Founded
Genesis

An Antidote to the Rushed Morning

Roast & Revel began with a simple observation: modern coffee had surrendered its soul to speed. The sacred ritual of pausing, inhaling the aroma of freshly ground beans, and conversing across a marble table had been replaced by drive-through windows and mass-roasted, bitter beans.

In 2018, we restored a historic brick space with high arched windows, commissioned a 12-kilogram cast-iron drum roaster, and committed ourselves to uncompromising quality. We source directly from growers we know by name, roast in small batches, and bake our pastries from scratch every dawn.

Here, every cup is poured with patience, precision, and gratitude for the long journey from seed to demitasse.

Guiding Values

Our Four Pillars

Every decision in our roastery and café is governed by these foundational convictions.

01

Terroir Before Technique

The finest barista cannot create sweetness that wasn't nurtured in the soil. We celebrate micro-climates, soil minerals, and botanical varietals rather than masking them.

02

The Pace of Slowness

Great coffee takes the time it takes. A slow-drip pour cannot be hurried without sacrificing clarity. We encourage our guests to linger, read, and converse without haste.

03

Classical Hospitality

Hospitality is an act of generous service. From remembering your favourite origin to warm greeting at dawn, we treat every patron as an honored guest in our home.

04

Culinary Integrity

No artificial syrups, no industrial pre-mixes. We use real Madagascar bourbon vanilla, single-estate dark chocolates, and authentic French AOP Lescure butter.

From Soil to Demitasse

Our Coffee-Making Process

Four disciplined stages of craftsmanship ensuring peerless cup quality.

Step 01

Direct Ethical Sourcing

Visiting estates at origin during harvest. We select only ripe, dense cherries harvested at 1,800m+ altitudes with documented water stewardship.

Step 02

Small-Batch Roasting

Cast-iron drum conduction profiles each origin individually, ensuring sweetness and floral acidity are unlocked without bitter carbon notes.

Step 03

Blind Cupping & QA

Every roast batch is cupped following strict Specialty Coffee Association (SCA) protocol. Only lots scoring 87+ points enter our espresso hoppers.

Step 04

Precision Culinary Extraction

Remineralized reverse-osmosis water, conical burr grinding within 0.1g tolerances, and multi-stage manual pour-overs calibrated daily.

With over 16 years behind the roasting drum in Oslo and San Francisco, Henrik specializes in gentle, high-clarity roast profiles that respect botanical terroir.

Normandy native trained at Ferrandi Paris. Antoine brings classical 72-hour cold fermentation and Lescure butter lamination to our dawn baking program.

Architecture & Space

An Architectural Sanctuary in the Heart of the City

Every material in Roast & Revel was selected for its tactile honesty. Rich dark walnut millwork provides acoustic warmth; unlacquered brass hardware captures golden afternoon light; and honed Carrara marble offers a cool, clean surface for your cup.

No blaring screens, no rushed assembly lines. Only classical ambient playlists, the hiss of steam, and generous seating designed for undisturbed hours of thought.
