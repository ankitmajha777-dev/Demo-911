Visiting Guidelines

Reservation Policies

  • •
    15-Minute Grace Period:

    Please notify us if delayed. After 15 minutes, unoccupied tables may be released to awaiting walk-ins.

  • •
    Walk-In Availability:

    We always keep 30% of our café tables available for spontaneous walk-in guests throughout the day.

  • •
    Private Cupping & Groups (8+):

    For private tasting events or roastery tours, email us at events@roastandrevel.coffee.
