Artisanal Science

Our Roasting Philosophy

The roaster is not an inventor of flavor, but a careful custodian. Our job is to gently coax the bean’s origin narrative into the open.

01

12kg Cast-Iron Drum

We roast exclusively in a vintage cast-iron drum roaster. Cast iron distributes thermal mass gently through conductive heat, preventing scorch marks and guaranteeing deep, uniform core development.

02

Real-Time RoR Profiling

Every roast is monitored second-by-second using dual thermocouple probes measuring Bean Temp and Exhaust Gas. A smooth, downward Rate of Rise curve preserves delicate terpenes and organic acids.

03

The 14-Day Resting Window

Contrary to popular belief, coffee should not be brewed immediately out of the roaster. We seal our beans in nitrogen-flushed valve bags and rest them 7 to 14 days so trapped CO2 subsides, revealing crystal-clear clarity.

Barista Academy

Master Brewing Guides

Precision recipes developed by our head of coffee for optimal extraction at home or at our brew bar.

Pour-Over Protocol

Hario V60 Precision

Best suited for light-roast floral Ethiopians and Colombian Geishas.

Coffee Dose15.0 grams
Water Mass250 grams (1:16.6)
Grind SizeMedium-Fine (Kosher Salt)
Water Temp92°C / 198°F
  1. Rinse paper filter thoroughly with hot water; discard rinse water.
  2. Bloom with 45g water for 45s, gently swirling the slurry.
  3. Pour in smooth concentric circles up to 150g by 1:15.
  4. Final continuous pour to 250g by 2:00. Total draw-down at 3:00.
Immersion Protocol

Artisanal French Press

Ideal for medium roasts from Guatemala and rich Sumatran lots.

Coffee Dose30.0 grams
Water Mass450 grams (1:15)
Grind SizeCoarse Sea Salt
Water Temp94°C / 201°F
  1. Add coarse grounds to pre-warmed carafe. Pour all 450g water rapidly.
  2. Leave untouched for 4 minutes to allow a natural coffee crust to form.
  3. Break the crust with two spoons, scoop off top foam and silver skin.
  4. Insert plunger just below surface, wait 4 minutes, and pour gently.
Pressure Protocol

Commercial 9-Bar Espresso

Our exacting standards for straight shots and milk pairings.

Dry Dose18.5 grams
Liquid Yield38.0 grams (1:2.05)
Pressure9.0 bars flat
Extraction27 — 29 seconds
  1. Distribute grounds evenly with WDT needle tool to avoid channeling.
  2. Tamp level at 15kg pressure with precision calibrated base.
  3. Pre-infuse at 3 bars for 4 seconds until first drops emerge.
  4. Ramp to 9 bars; target golden tiger-stripe crema and velvety mouthfeel.
Ethical Sourcing

Generational Relationships, Fair Pricing

Every bean in our roastery tells a story of the person who tended the soil. We refuse commodity exchange pricing and instead contract directly with independent washing stations and smallholder cooperatives.

By paying 80% to 120% above Fair Trade minimums, our partner producers invest in clean river filtration, living wages for pickers, and biodiversity preservation in sensitive volcanic cloud forests.
